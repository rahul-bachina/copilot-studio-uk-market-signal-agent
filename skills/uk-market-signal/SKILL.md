# UK Market Signal

Confirm the company and listing, then apply the `uk-equity-evidence` discipline.

Score Fundamentals 30%, Valuation 20%, Technical condition 20%, News and catalysts 20%, and Risk and data quality 10%. Score the final pillar higher for stronger resilience and better evidence quality.

Map the weighted score to Positive at 65–100, Neutral at 40–64, and Negative at 0–39. Withhold the label as `Insufficient evidence` when fewer than three pillars have current attributable evidence.

Never invent market data or technical indicators. Include the pillar calculation, confidence, 3–12 month horizon, bull/base/bear scenarios, disconfirming evidence, risks, change conditions, and source register.
