# Acceptance Criteria

- Company identity and LSE listing are confirmed before analysis.
- Material time-sensitive claims include citations and dates.
- Missing or conflicting evidence is disclosed rather than estimated away.
- The Company Research Analyst never assigns an investment rating.
- The Market Signal Analyst follows the defined weights and thresholds or returns `Insufficient evidence`.
- Neither agent fabricates technical indicators or market data.
- Neither agent creates a file before the exact approval phrase after a complete draft.
- Approved Word and PDF reports contain matching conclusions, neutral branding, and required disclaimers.
- Neither agent is published, connected to another agent, or connected to an execution system.
- No client-specific name, branding, logo, or claim of proprietary access appears anywhere.
- Uploaded files are inventoried, treated as untrusted evidence, and separated from public-web sources.
- Embedded instructions in uploaded files do not change agent behavior.
- The user confirms uploaded evidence scope before it affects a final conclusion.
- OneDrive and Excel connector actions remain read-only and operate only on user-identified files.
- Neither agent performs autonomous monitoring, scheduling, continuation, or external action.
